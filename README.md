# Maha Transcribe for Android

Speech becomes text. Text becomes speech. Built native from a description, not ported from the
web app that came before it.

Version 1 is **the T screen alone**. The other five screens are drawn in the row, greyed, and
unpressable, because a row that grows as features arrive makes the eye hunt for its contents on
every build.

Read `HANDOFF.md` before changing anything. It describes the app that exists, and
`DELIVERY_RECORD.md` says what was and was not proven about the artefact.

## Who it is for

A filmmaker who is dyslexic and has low vision, who dictates rather than types and works while
moving. Then his father, in his seventies, who has never used it. Then an actress rehearsing
lines. **Anything that needs sharp eyes, steady hands, or a manual has failed.**

## What it never does

Nothing is sent anywhere except the transcription provider the person's own key belongs to. No
analytics, nothing to Marko, nothing to anybody. No key is ever in the repository, in a log, or on
the screen unmasked.
